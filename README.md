# The-Job-Search

<img width="5184" height="3456" alt="the job seach" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5c935cbd-6505-4562-b0eb-1525c4fca928" />

The Job Search is an art installation piece that resembles a classic Macintosh computer, featuring a parodied version of The Oregon Trail game, taking users through the journey of interviewing for a job by depicting the harshness, unpredictability, and nonsense of the interview process. This work invites viewers to interact with, and play the game in which they determine their background, advantages, and make choices in the hopes of landing a job. Whether they realize it or not, the game is full of research backed prejudice, unfairness, and uncontrollable variables of the job market. Through their choices the users will find if they land the job or get dropped from the process with a final page that informs them of the factors unseen to them that were affecting the process. This work is designed to be installed in a gallery, museum, or public space where any user can play, with the aim to use dark comedy and real experiences to inform users of the variables at play in today’s job application process.
